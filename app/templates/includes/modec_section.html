Peptide Motif Deconvolution (MoDec)


ⓘ Peptides were deconvolved into binding motifs with MoDec after removal of peptides labelled as #CONTAM or #DECOY, PTMs and resulting duplicates. The analysis uses 12–20-mers (duplicates removed). MoDec finds the motifs and the binding core of every peptide without prior alignment, and also fits one flat motif that absorbs peptides that fit no specific motif (often contaminants). Choose how many motifs to show per replicate; the number with the lowest AIC (marked on the graph) is shown first, but AIC is only a guide, so please inspect the motifs. The percentage under each logo is the share of peptides whose best-matching motif it is. Under each logo are the best-matching source allotypes and their PCC (Pearson Correlation Coefficient), as determined by MHC-TP; for the comparison, MoDec's frequencies are converted to log-odds scores. Note: MHC Class II allotype predictions are in preview — results should be interpreted with caution.
MoDec: Racle et al., Nat Biotechnol 2019;37:1283–1286. MoDec was made available through the generosity of the Ludwig Institute for Cancer Research Ltd, for academic non-commercial research use.
